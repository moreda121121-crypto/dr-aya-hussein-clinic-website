How Much Water Does Your Pet Need?

Learn the right amount of water for your dog or cat to keep them healthy and active.

Most dogs need roughly 50 ml of water per kg of body weight each day. Cats need a little less, around 40–50 ml per kg, and more if they eat dry food.

Keep fresh water in a clean bowl and refill it daily. Several bowls around the house encourage cats to drink.

Hot weather, exercise and illness raise needs. If your pet suddenly drinks much more or much less than usual, book a checkup.

Tips to Keep Your Cat Warm in Winter

Simple tips to protect your cat from the cold and keep them cozy at home.

Give your cat a soft bed or blanket away from drafts and cold floors.

Keep cats indoors on cold nights, and check under car hoods in the morning if outdoor cats visit.

Older cats and kittens feel the cold most. Slightly more food in winter helps them keep warm.

Daily Care Tips for a Healthy Dog

Essential daily care tips for a happy, healthy and long life.

Walk your dog daily and give them time to play and sniff.

Brush their coat often and check ears, eyes and paws.

Brush teeth several times a week and keep vaccinations and parasite control on schedule.

Nov 23, 2026

Best Nutrition for Your Pet

Discover the right food and nutrition tips for your beloved pet.

Choose a complete food made for your pet's age, size and species. Puppies and kittens need growth formulas.

Measure portions instead of free feeding to avoid weight gain.

Change foods slowly over 7–10 days, and ask us before starting a special diet.
